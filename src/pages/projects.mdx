<Grid cols={2}>
  <Card title="GTOC-14" tag="Competition · forming interest">
    The Global Trajectory Optimization Competition, often called the "America's Cup of Rocket Science," is an
    international trajectory-optimization contest. GTOC-14 hasn't been announced yet, but NSL intends to field a team
    and is gathering interested members ahead of the announcement.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Open-Source Star Tracker Software" tag="In development">
    An open attitude-determination algorithm that identifies a spacecraft's orientation from the stars it sees. A
    Software team project.
  </Card>

  <Card title="SatSim" tag="Early development">
    An open-source Python framework for end-to-end CubeSat mission simulation, with modules for orbital mechanics,
    spacecraft dynamics, and mission analysis. The goal is a tool people actually use for mission design and teaching.
    It's the Simulations team's mission sandbox.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Deployable Solar Panel System" tag="In development" meta="PEAK funded">
    A student-designed deployable solar-panel mechanism that gets more power out of a CubeSat's small surface area.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Pointing & Tracking System" tag="Apply to join" meta="With PhD students">
    Pointing and tracking development alongside Northeastern PhD students. Spots are limited, so members apply to join.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Cornell-Style ChipSats" tag="Up next">
    Chip-scale satellites in the style of Cornell's ChipSats, where the whole spacecraft fits on one small circuit
    board. A new Hardware project that's just getting started.
  </Card>
